- `the set graphics-context Odyssey driver request` in `builds/vpro-patch-4103/usr/cpu/sysgen/IP30boot/odyssey.a(odsy_tport.o)` at 0x30
  **Inferred.** Writes a graphics-context request to the Odyssey port at a0+0x110000 as a run of 32-bit words. It makes no calls. Every store goes either to that port or to the driver state block whose pointer is loaded from the GP slot at -0x7800, so the state offsets below are relative to that block. It starts by emitting literal command words with high half 0x0001, such as 0x10443, 0x10046, 0x10019 and 0x10424. Paths then branch on a4 (t0, compared with 2 and tested for zero), a6 (t2, tested for zero) and a7 (t3, tested for zero). On the main path a6 is reset to 0x96, bits 3 and 4 are set in the state word at +0x84, and +0xbc is set to 0x200. Fields of the stack argument at 0x54(sp) go into +0xc0 and +0xc4. Six words of a table are then read at offsets 0x4118 to 0x412c from the GP slot at -0x77f8. The table row is selected by 24 times a4, and its fields are packed into one doubleword at 0(sp) with bit 29 set. The fields come from bits 4-6, 7-9, 10-12, 13-15 and 19. The low word is stored at +0x1b2c, and the words at +0x1b24 to +0x1b30 are written to the port. The routine ends after a 0x50-byte frame.
