# Video format compiler rules of the VPro install

The files under `usr/gfx/ucode/vfc/rules/` are the input rules of the video format compiler that produced every `.vfo` of [display-files.md](display-files.md): text in the compiler's own rule language, shipped with the install. They state, in the vendor's own arithmetic, how the register words of the clock parts, the timing generator, the digital output path and the genlock logic follow from a format's raster and frame rate. Every statement below is **established** by reading the whole file; the 6.5.30m install is the source. Byte offsets are from the start of each file. A check against compiled output: for `1280x1024_60.vfo` the two clock-part words `ODY_AMI_PLL_0x0` to `ODY_AMI_PLL_0x4` hold 153, 6, 1, 92 and 59, so the reference divider is 6 x 256 + 153 = 1689 and the feedback divider 59 x 256 + 92 = 15196, and 12 MHz x 15196 / 1689 is 107.9645 MHz, the file's `AchievedFrequency` of 107.964476.

- `usr/gfx/ucode/vfc/rules/VPro_Board.def`: **established.**
  The board description: 1503 lines, a chip-independent set of rules for the whole VPro output stage. It holds two clock-part searches, a block `define board_hardware` that wires timing-generator outputs to named signals, and a run of post-processing blocks that turn the raster into register words.
  The first search starts at offset 46. It fixes the input frequency at 12 MHz, allows a reference divider from 3 to 129 and a feedback divider from 12 to 4103 and a post scaler of 2, 4, 8 or 16, requires the loop oscillator between 125 and 500 MHz and the phase-detector input between 0.008 and 0.500 MHz, and keeps the setting with the smallest frequency error and then the fewest set divider bits (the penalty counts powers of two the dividers exceed). The result goes out as `ODY_PLL_FdBkDiv0` (feedback minus 8, low byte), `ODY_PLL_FdBkDiv1` (the next four bits), `ODY_PLL_Osc_Div` (128 plus the reference divider minus 2) and the post scaler as bits 5:4 of `ODY_PLL_LoopControl`, which starts at 2 (post scaler 2 adds 0, 4 adds 0x10, 8 adds 0x20, 16 adds 0x30). `ICS_AchievedFrequency` records what the setting gives.
  The second search starts at offset 4954. It searches reference dividers from 4096 down, three post-divider fields (two bits, two bits and one bit, selecting 1, 2, 4 or 8 and factors of 3, 5 and 4) and a feedback divider up to 16384, with the loop oscillator between 40 and 230 MHz and a feedback word whose upper bits are not smaller than its low three bits. It packs the winner into eight words: `ODY_AMI_PLL_0x0` the low byte and `ODY_AMI_PLL_0x1` the high nibble of the reference divider, `ODY_AMI_PLL_0x2` the three post-divider fields (bits 5:4, 3:2 and 1:0), `ODY_AMI_PLL_0x3` and `ODY_AMI_PLL_0x4` the low byte and the high six bits of the feedback divider, `ODY_AMI_PLL_0x5` equal to 0 when the oscillator is above 140 MHz and 0x10 otherwise, and the last two zero. It sets `AchievedFrequency` and, when the achieved frame rate is within one per cent of the requested one, replaces `FramesPerSecond` with it.
  The block at offset 10836 names the signals the timing generator drives and the chip output each one uses: `MON_HCSYNC`, `MON_VSYNC`, `XTK_KSYNC`, `STEREO`, `DAC_CSYNC_N` and `DAC_CBLANK_N` on outputs C0 to C5, `OPT_HDRIVE` on C6, then `OPT_VDRIVE`, `GEN_PDEN`, `LOC_HSYNC`, `CURS_HPOS`, `CURS_VPOS`, `DMA_RESET_N` and `HIF_H_UPDATE_EN` on B7 to B13 and `VINT`, `SWP_RDY_LCK`, `END_OF_LINE`, `HIF_V_UPDATE_EN`, `XMP_CBLANK_N`, `END_OF_FIELD` and `REQUEST_SRC` on A14 to A20. The rules at offset 12557 then place each edge from the format's porches: the monitor sync pair rises at the start of each line for the sync time and at each vertical sync, the pixel-request signal pulses high for 64 pixels of every 128, 98 pixels before the pixels it asks for, and `END_OF_LINE` pulses for pixels 4 to 8 after the start of active video, shifted by the number of pixels needed to round the line up to a multiple of 64.
  The blocks from offset 31967 on set the output limits (the pixel clock must lie between 12 and 202.6 MHz, the DAC clock below 240 MHz, the width between 640 and 4096 pixels, the height between 480 and 2048 lines and, for two-channel formats, a multiple of 64 pixels wide), the display-bus clock divider (`ODY_DCBClockSpeed`, from 266 MHz divided by 8, 16, 32 or 64, lowered until it is at most 40 MHz), the two-wire clock dividers (`ODY_I2CClockSpeed` and `ODY_DDCClockSpeed`, chosen so the bus runs below 0.4 MHz and 0.1 MHz), the timing generator words (`ODY_VTG_Control` as 0x1F0 plus a frame-reset delay in bits 21:9, `ODY_VTG_InitState` 0, `ODY_VTG_ChanEn` 0x3fffff), the output-path words (`ODY_DAC_Cntrl`, `ODY_DP_Cntrl`, `ODY_SM_Cntrl` fixed at 0x10120000) and the porch counts in pixels. `ODY_DP_Cntrl` always has bits 1, 8 and 9 set; bit 2 is added when `TTL_Sync` is nonzero, bit 3 or bits 5:4 for the other `TTL_Sync` bits, bit 6 for stereo, bit 7 for inverted stereo and bits 11:10 for RS-343A levels.
  The block at offset 39931 derives the genlock words from the format's scan type (`GENScanFormat` 1 to 5, each with its own enable-pulse polarity, lines-to-gain percentage and detect patterns): `ODY_GEN_lPFD` and `ODY_GEN_hPFD` for the frame detector, `ODY_GEN_lPSD` and `ODY_GEN_hPSD` for the phase detector (frame duration in display-bus clocks, 3 frames to gain and 6 to lose lock), `ODY_GEN_lHMASK` and `ODY_GEN_hHMASK` for the horizontal mask, `ODY_GEN_BP_CLAMP`, `ODY_GENLOCK` and `ODY_GEN_CONTROL`, then the genlock clock part's words `GEN_AMI_REG1` (0xC0), `GEN_AMI_REG2`, `GEN_AMI_REG5` and `GEN_AMI_REG6` (0x8E) from a charge-pump current computed from the line rate. The last two blocks set `FormatInfo_DualHead` and `FormatInfo_DigitalSkew` from the option name and leave eight two-channel clock words (`ODY_2CHANNEL_PLL_reg0` to `ODY_2CHANNEL_PLL_reg7`) and the six FPGA words for another tool to fill; they are zero in every shipped file.
- `usr/gfx/ucode/vfc/rules/VPro_Chip.def`: **established.**
  Names the timing generator part and its limits in 189 bytes. It defines the chip hardware `vtg` with the exported string `VTGHardwareName` set to "VTG1", a longest line of 32768 clocks (`LineLengthMaximum`) and at most 16 fields (`FieldCountMaximum`), the values that every compiled `.vfo` of the VPro board carries at the same names.
- `usr/gfx/ucode/vfc/rules/VPro_BlockSync.def`: **established.**
  A template for the plain formats, 4692 bytes, with the compiler's parameter list in its opening comment (`FORMAT_NAME`, `F_FRM_PER_SEC`, the active lines and pixels, the three vertical and three horizontal porch sizes). Its `General` block at offset 395 sets the field count from `FIELDS_PER_FRAME` (default 1), makes the total lines the field count times the sum of the porch, sync and active lines, and the total pixels the sum of the horizontal porch, sync, back porch and active pixels (a skew-mode switch fixes the three at 20, 24 and 20). Its `Active Line` block at offset 909 exports the vertical porches and the `DCDHorizontal...` pixel counts and sets `DCDSupport` to 1. Three `Field` blocks (offsets 1715, 2998 and 3794) describe the vertical sync as a low first line, a run of unchanged lines and a last line that restores the level, then back-porch, active and front-porch lines that each start with a low pulse of the horizontal sync time and are high for the rest of the line.
- `usr/gfx/ucode/vfc/rules/VPro_DCD.def`: **established.**
  The dual-channel template, 4354 bytes, the same layout as the block-sync template with the option name `DUAL_CHANNEL` set on its first line. Its `General` block at offset 141 fixes two fields per frame and makes the total lines twice the sum of one field's porch, sync and active lines. Its two `Field` blocks (offsets 1427 and 2839) differ only in the vertical sync: the first (a six-line sync) drives the line low for five lines and releases it for one horizontal front porch before the back porch, the second drops the level on its first line, holds it for the sync length less two lines and then restores it, as the block-sync template does. The horizontal counts in `Active Line` at offset 628 are the same `DCD` exports as in the block-sync template.
