# Stored published sources

Full copies of the published patents, papers, technical reports, manuals and white papers that [../published-sources.md](published-sources.md) cites for VPro (the BUZZ and PB&J chips). They are third-party documents kept here so a reader does not depend on a link that may vanish. Each entry in published-sources.md gives the in-repo path and the SHA-256 of its copy.

Layout: `patents/` by US patent number; `papers/` for conference and journal papers and slide decks; `reports/` for SGI technical reports and white papers; `manuals/` for SGI manuals, named by document number; `specs/` for datasheets and brochures.

The `sgi-docs` submodule holds only the IRIS GL manuals and a GFO format note; it has no hardware, VPro, Octane2, Fuel or Tezro documents, so every VPro document is stored here in full.

Each copy is the file as fetched from the URL below, checked against its SHA-256; where no fetch URL was recorded, the row gives the document's title instead. A copy that was not on its original host any more comes from the Wayback Machine capture named in the URL.
