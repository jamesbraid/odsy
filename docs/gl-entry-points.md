# Public GL entry points and the command words their senders emit

This chapter maps public OpenGL vertex-attribute entry points to the command words their Odyssey senders store, with the address of each sender and of the instruction that forms its word. Build: IRIX 6.5.30m, N32 `libGLcore.so` for IP30 (`builds/6.5.30-media/usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`). The attribute word layout is in [tokens.md](tokens.md#attribute-words); the words here are the 32-bit values that layout gives for each sender.

Two sender families appear. An immediate-mode sender, named `__glOdyim_*`, stores its word and operands straight to the port at absolute address 0x00010000. A display-list sender, named `__glOdylc_*`, stores its word and operands into a list-space record at the list pointer, and some paths call `__glSPDlistSpace` for room first.

Method: each word is the value of the `lui` and `ori` pair at the address given, read from the routine's N32 disassembly. Operand order comes from the store instructions, and the routine's address and size come from the function table. Labels follow [tokens.md](tokens.md): **established** for what was read in the instructions named, **inferred** for what was read but not traced to a meaning.

Mapping from each sender to its public entry point: for the immediate-mode senders the public implementation is in the public tree, `ogl-sample/gfx/lib/opengl/glcore/s_capi.c` (for example `__glim_Color3ui` at line 221), so the name correspondence is **established**. For the display-list senders no public list-compile routine for colour was found under `ogl-sample/gfx/lib/opengl/glcore/` (a search for `Color3ui` matched only `s_capi.c` and `s_varray.c`), so their correspondence to the public entry point is **inferred** from the `__glOdylc_` prefix and the name.

## Immediate-mode senders

- `__glOdyim_Color3ui` (`builds/6.5.30-media/usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`, 0x0dad4700, 32 bytes): the sender of glColor3ui in immediate mode. The word `0xc580cc0a` comes from `lui a7,0xc580` at 0x0dad4700 and `ori a7,a7,0xcc0a` at 0x0dad4708. The word is stored first, at 0x0dad470c, then a0, a1 and a2 at 0x0dad4710, 0x0dad4714 and 0x0dad471c (the last in the delay slot of `jr ra` at 0x0dad4718).
