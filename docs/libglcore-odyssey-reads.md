# Odyssey driver routines of `libGLcore.so`, read one by one

Release IRIX 6.5.30m, N32 `libGLcore.so` for IP30 (`usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`; the IP35 copy and the patch builds carry the same routines where their code hashes agree in `docs/inventory/functions.tsv`). Each entry names a routine of the board-specific `ODYSSEY` directory (`libglcore/asm/ODYSSEY/`), its address and size, and says what it computes. Labels are **established** for routines whose whole body was read in the Ghidra decompilation (`$gp` and jump tables resolved with `ghidra/scripts/IrixGp.java` and `IrixJumpTables.java`) next to the assembly, and **inferred** for statements that rest on a callee that was not read. Offsets are into the Odyssey GL context record (the record the library keeps its current context in, address 0x1010 of the process), unless an entry says another record. The routines that move pixels, draw and copy pixel rectangles, transfer depth and stencil, and set the raster position belong to other chapters and are not read here.

Words sent to the board are the class-1 command words of `BUZZ.h`: `0x00010000 | COUNT << 10 | REG`, followed by COUNT operands. "The settle sequence" is the group `0x10443` with operand 250, `0x10046` twice and `0x10019`, `0x10443` with operand 150 and `0x10046` four times, and `0x10443` with operand 250 and `0x10046` twice, as in `docs/opengl.md` (the X module's draw routines send the same words). Writes go to the command-FIFO window the library maps at process address 0x10000.

Dispatch-table slots. The context holds a pointer at +0x17f4 to a table of 4-byte entry points in the order of the GL entry names (`__glim_Vertex2f` calls slot +0x534, the `Vertex2fv` entry, with a pointer to two floats). Names given for slots below are **inferred** from that order together with the width and count of the arguments each caller passes.

## Validation and defaults

- `__glOdysseyUpdateTransform` (`builds/6.5.30-media/usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`, 0xd9e2980, 1544 bytes): **established.**
  Analyses the current projection matrix and stores the eye-space distances the depth mapping needs. It reads the projection matrix record at +0x741c and the model-view record at +0x7414. When the byte at +0x19dcc is set, the projection's flag word at +0x40 is 4 and the model-view record has a nonzero term at +0xc, +0x1c or +0x2c, it copies the 4 x 4 at +0xb0 of that record with its fourth column set to 0, 0, 0 and 1.0, takes its inverse transpose with `__glInvertTransposeMatrix`, multiplies the projection's fourth row (+0xc, +0x1c, +0x2c, +0x3c) through it to get the four terms used below, and sets the byte at +0x19dcd to 1. Otherwise it clears that byte and reads the matrix terms directly, replacing a projection with a skew by its scale factors, the square roots of the sums of squares of the column terms at +0x8, +0x18 and +0x28 and at +0xc, +0x1c and +0x2c, signed by the sign of the diagonal entries +0x28 and +0x2c.
  The four numbers it ends with (the two scales and the two translations) are cached in doubles at +0x165f8, +0x16600, +0x16608 and +0x16610 and the routine reuses them without recomputing when the terms at +0x28, +0x2c, +0x38 and +0x3c already equal the cached values and the terms at +0x8, +0xc, +0x18 and +0x1c are zero. It sets the word at +0x1673c to 1 when the matrix at +0x7428 has a nonzero perspective column, clears the byte at +0x16740, calls the unnamed routine at 0xd9e4d90, and calls `__glOdyim_PolygonOffsetEXT` again when either of the offset factors at +0x1f4 and +0x1f8 is nonzero, so that the polygon offset follows the new depth scale.

- `__glOdysseyValidateFog` (`builds/6.5.30-media/usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`, 0xd980cd0, 1800 bytes): **established.**
  Turns the GL fog state into the three fog words of block 0x1a (+0x7c10, +0x7c14, +0x7c18 and +0x7c1c) and marks dirty bit 8. An offset below -8 or above 7.996 is brought into range by the exponent, and one beyond -1024 or 1023.5 is replaced by -8 or 7.996 with the scale reduced by the same ratio.

## Primitive replay for the clip path

- `__glOdysseyVertexWARClip` (`builds/6.5.30-media/usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`, 0xd9ef330, 4452 bytes): **established.**
  Called with one vertex (four float arguments) when a primitive started on the board must be finished through the software clip path; it ends the board's primitive, rebuilds the transform state the software path needs, and re-issues the primitive's held vertices through the immediate-mode entry points, ending with the vertex it was called for. It first asks the hardware-shadow routine for the board's primitive state (`__glOdysseyGetHardwareShadow`) and copies 0x900 bytes of it to its stack. The word at +0x80 of that copy holds the primitive mode in bits 3:0 (a value of 10 or more makes it print a message and send nothing), the number of vertices held in bits 5:4, the index of the oldest held vertex in bits 7:6 (the held vertices form a ring of four) and a strip-parity flag in bit 11.
  It sets the byte at +0x169fe, for mode 2 sets the settle flag at +0x9830 and sends `0x10444` with operand 0, calls the dispatch table's end entry (+0x2c, inferred End) and zeroes the pair at +0x80 and +0x84 of the record at +0x6d4. The words at +0x88 and +0x8c of that record take -1.0 and 1.0 when the byte at +0x19dcd is set, and otherwise a unit step whose sign follows the order of the far and near distances at +0x165d0 and +0x165c8, and that step multiplied by n. When the projection class at +0x19dbc is 3 it also writes four clip planes into the record at +0x6d4, from +0x90 to +0xcc: for the x and y rows of the projection matrix at +0x741c the planes are 0.9 times the row's x (or y) term plus its w term, and w minus 0.9 times the term, so they sit at 1/0.9 of the viewing volume's x and y bounds (the constant is 0.9 in the library's double pool at 0xdaf4fc0).
