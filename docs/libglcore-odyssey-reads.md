# Odyssey driver routines of `libGLcore.so`, read one by one

Release IRIX 6.5.30m, N32 `libGLcore.so` for IP30 (`usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`; the IP35 copy and the patch builds carry the same routines where their code hashes agree in `docs/inventory/functions.tsv`). Each entry names a routine of the board-specific `ODYSSEY` directory (`libglcore/asm/ODYSSEY/`), its address and size, and says what it computes. Labels are **established** for routines whose whole body was read in the Ghidra decompilation (`$gp` and jump tables resolved with `ghidra/scripts/IrixGp.java` and `IrixJumpTables.java`) next to the assembly, and **inferred** for statements that rest on a callee that was not read. Offsets are into the Odyssey GL context record (the record the library keeps its current context in, address 0x1010 of the process), unless an entry says another record. The routines that move pixels, draw and copy pixel rectangles, transfer depth and stencil, and set the raster position belong to other chapters and are not read here.

Words sent to the board are the class-1 command words of `BUZZ.h`: `0x00010000 | COUNT << 10 | REG`, followed by COUNT operands. "The settle sequence" is the group `0x10443` with operand 250, `0x10046` twice and `0x10019`, `0x10443` with operand 150 and `0x10046` four times, and `0x10443` with operand 250 and `0x10046` twice, as in `docs/opengl.md` (the X module's draw routines send the same words). Writes go to the command-FIFO window the library maps at process address 0x10000.

Dispatch-table slots. The context holds a pointer at +0x17f4 to a table of 4-byte entry points in the order of the GL entry names (`__glim_Vertex2f` calls slot +0x534, the `Vertex2fv` entry, with a pointer to two floats). Names given for slots below are **inferred** from that order together with the width and count of the arguments each caller passes.

## Validation and defaults

- `__glOdysseyValidateHardware` (`builds/6.5.30-media/usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`, 0xd968180, 4992 bytes): **established.**
  Pushes every piece of changed context state to the board as class-1 blocks, choosing the blocks by the dirty word at +0x16960 (read at entry; set to 0 at exit). Before each block it tests the "settle needed" word at +0x9830 and, when set, clears it and sends the settle prefix `0x10443` 250, `0x10046`, `0x10046`. The bit-to-block map, each block's operands taken from the context at the offset shown: bit 1 sends `0x10404` with the word at +0x7bfc and bit 2 sends `0x10405` with the word at +0x7c00; bit 0 sends `0x10c21` with three words from +0x7c34; bit 3 sends `0x1181a` with six words from +0x7c08; bit 4 sends `0x1081d` with the two words at +0x7c20; bit 5 sends `0x1041f` with the word at +0x7c2c; bit 6 sends `0x1084a` with the two words at +0x7c54; bit 7 sends `0x10452` with the word at +0x7c5c; bit 8 sends `0x1141b` with five words from +0x7cb0; bit 9 sends `0x11c16` with seven words from +0x7cc4; bit 13 sends `0x11847` with six words from +0x970c; bit 14 sends `0x11013` with four words from +0x9724; bit 15 sends `0x10c3e` with three words from +0x8d6c.
  The wider groups: bit 10 sends 64 level descriptors, blocks 0x280 to 0x2bf of three words each from +0x7ce0 (twelve-byte stride); bit 11 sends `0x10406` with the word at +0x7fe0; bit 12 sends the settle sequence, `0x10018` with no operand, and the settle sequence again; bits 18, 19 and 20 each send sixteen blocks of sixteen words, blocks 0x220 to 0x22f from +0x7fe4, 0x230 to 0x23f from +0x83e4 and 0x240 to 0x24f from +0x87e4; bit 22 sends two runs of sixteen blocks of sixteen words, blocks 0x200 to 0x20f from +0x8d7c and 0x210 to 0x21f from +0x917c; bit 23 sends `0x10c1c` with three words from +0x957c; bit 24 sends `0x10451` with the word at +0x9698; bit 25 sends `0x10415` with the word at +0x7c04; bit 21 sends the settle sequence and then either `0x90008116` or, when bit 27 of the word at +0x96a4 is set, blocks 0x041, 0x04b and 0x040 with zero operands (the second with 0x00ffffff in its second operand), followed by `0x11017` with four words from +0x969c.

Bit 16 reads the mode at +0x8ce8: mode 1 sends blocks 0x030 to 0x033 of eight words each from +0x8cec (0x20-byte stride), mode 2 sends block 0x03b with eight words from +0x8cec, mode 3 sends `0x1083d` with two words.   The contents of blocks 0x028 to 0x02b, 0x038 and 0x03a are **unknown**; `docs/buzz.md` (the second three-layout set) lists the routes tried.

- `__glOdysseyValidateDispatchTable` (`builds/6.5.30-media/usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`, 0xd970d30, 1844 bytes): **established.**
  Chooses which of the library's entry-point tables the context's dispatch table at +0x17f4 is filled with, from the clip state, and copies it in. It reads the number of software clip planes through `__glOdysseyGetNumSWClipPlanes` and, when +0xc84 is 0x1c00, calls `__glOdysseyValidateSoftClipPlanes`. The result is a mode number kept at +0x1695c: 1 when +0xc84 is not 0x1c00; otherwise 0 (no software clip planes, word at +0x16980 zero), 3 (no software clip planes, that word nonzero), 2 (software clip planes, that word zero) or 4 (software clip planes, that word nonzero). It returns early when the mode is unchanged and the byte at +0x19dc0 is clear.
  The table is one of three sets (modes 0 and 3, modes 2 and 4, or mode 1, which uses a single table), and within a set one of four tables chosen by the byte at +0xc94 (colour-index or RGBA visual) and the word at +0x16980. The routine copies it with `__glMipsCopyDispatch` and calls two unnamed helpers at 0xd9704c0 and 0xd970bd0 around the copy (not read). For mode 0 it then refreshes the projection class at +0x19dbc when the byte at +0x19dc0 is set (0 when the byte at +0x19db4 is clear, 3 when the word at +0x19db8 is 0x1102, else 2, lowered to 1 when class 2 has zero terms at +0xbc, +0xcc and +0xdc of the matrix record and the magnitude of +0xec exceeds that of the float at +0x19d90) and overwrites the 24 vertex entries (+0x528 to +0x584, Vertex2d through Vertex4sv): class 2 takes `__glOdyim_vertexSmallWDispatchTable`, class 1 replaces the eight Vertex4 entries with the `_smallw` forms, class 3 takes `__glOdyim_vertexMagDispatchTable` when bit 22 of the word at +0x7b70 is set and `__glOdyim_vertexMagLine0DispatchTable` otherwise. For modes 2 and 4 it points the context's primitive-begin entries (+0x2f34 to +0x2f58) at the soft-clip forms (`__glOdysseySoftClipBeginPoints` and its siblings) and the procedure pickers (+0x2e48 to +0x2e94) at the soft-clip pickers, using the fast triangle picker when the bytes at +0x19de2 and +0xc94 allow. For the other modes it writes the generic `__glBegin*` entries and pickers. It ends by setting the global at address 0x1018 to 2 and bit 0 of the word at +0x2e24, which flag that validation has more to do.
- `__glOdysseyValidateMatrixComb` (`builds/6.5.30-media/usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`, 0xd9e2f90, 1652 bytes): **established.**
  Rebuilds the combined viewport and projection numbers the board needs after a transform or viewport change. It forms two four-float vectors at +0x9588 to +0x95a4 from the matrix record at +0x7414 and the viewport words at +0x6b8 to +0x6c4, calls `__glOdysseyUpdateTransform` first when the byte at +0x16740 asks for it, and then computes, from the near and far distances at +0x165c8 and +0x165d0, the cached scale terms at +0x165f8 to +0x16610 and the words at +0x6c8 and +0x6cc, the rows stored at +0x95a8 to +0x95d4 (each a combination of the matrix's third and fourth columns, +0xb8 to +0xec of the record); the matrix's fourth-column terms +0xbc, +0xcc, +0xdc and +0xec are copied unchanged to +0x95b8 to +0x95c4.
  It then sets the projection class at +0x19dbc (the same classes `__glOdysseyValidateDispatchTable` reads): when the byte at +0x19dc0 is set it takes 0 if the byte at +0x19db4 is clear, 3 if the word at +0x19db8 is 0x1102 and 2 otherwise. When +0x19db4 is set and +0x19db8 is 0x1101 it picks 2 if any of +0xbc, +0xcc and +0xdc is nonzero, and when all three are zero it picks 2 or 1 by comparing the magnitude of +0xec with the float at +0x19d90, setting the word at +0x16738 to 1 in the all-zero cases. A change of class sets the byte at +0x19dc0 and bit 10 of +0x16964. It sets bit 4 of +0x16988 for classes 2 and 3 and clears it otherwise, ORs 1 and a comparison of the doubles at +0x6a8 and +0x6b0 (shifted to bit 1) into +0x9698, marks dirty bit 24 of +0x16960 so that block `0x10451` is resent, and finishes by sending the program-table word `0x34008000` built from the shadow at +0xa224 (the value is sent as 0, then 0x10, then 0).
- `__glOdysseyUpdateTransform` (`builds/6.5.30-media/usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`, 0xd9e2980, 1544 bytes): **established.**
  Analyses the current projection matrix and stores the eye-space distances the depth mapping needs. It reads the projection matrix record at +0x741c and the model-view record at +0x7414. When the byte at +0x19dcc is set, the projection's flag word at +0x40 is 4 and the model-view record has a nonzero term at +0xc, +0x1c or +0x2c, it copies the 4 x 4 at +0xb0 of that record with its fourth column set to 0, 0, 0 and 1.0, takes its inverse transpose with `__glInvertTransposeMatrix`, multiplies the projection's fourth row (+0xc, +0x1c, +0x2c, +0x3c) through it to get the four terms used below, and sets the byte at +0x19dcd to 1. Otherwise it clears that byte and reads the matrix terms directly, replacing a projection with a skew by its scale factors, the square roots of the sums of squares of the column terms at +0x8, +0x18 and +0x28 and at +0xc, +0x1c and +0x2c, signed by the sign of the diagonal entries +0x28 and +0x2c.
  The four numbers it ends with (the two scales and the two translations) are cached in doubles at +0x165f8, +0x16600, +0x16608 and +0x16610 and the routine reuses them without recomputing when the terms at +0x28, +0x2c, +0x38 and +0x3c already equal the cached values and the terms at +0x8, +0xc, +0x18 and +0x1c are zero. From them it derives the near and far distances n and f and writes them at +0x165c8 and +0x165d0, picking between the formulas for an orthographic matrix (the second scale zero) and a perspective matrix (the sum and difference of the scales). It sets the word at +0x1673c to 1 when the matrix at +0x7428 has a nonzero perspective column, clears the byte at +0x16740, calls the unnamed routine at 0xd9e4d90, and calls `__glOdyim_PolygonOffsetEXT` again when either of the offset factors at +0x1f4 and +0x1f8 is nonzero, so that the polygon offset follows the new depth scale.

## Primitive replay for the clip path

- `__glOdysseyVertexWARClip` (`builds/6.5.30-media/usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`, 0xd9ef330, 4452 bytes): **established.**
  Called with one vertex (four float arguments) when a primitive started on the board must be finished through the software clip path; it ends the board's primitive, rebuilds the transform state the software path needs, and re-issues the primitive's held vertices through the immediate-mode entry points, ending with the vertex it was called for. It first asks the hardware-shadow routine for the board's primitive state (`__glOdysseyGetHardwareShadow`) and copies 0x900 bytes of it to its stack. The word at +0x80 of that copy holds the primitive mode in bits 3:0 (a value of 10 or more makes it print a message and send nothing), the number of vertices held in bits 5:4, the index of the oldest held vertex in bits 7:6 (the held vertices form a ring of four) and a strip-parity flag in bit 11.
  It sets the byte at +0x169fe, for mode 2 sets the settle flag at +0x9830, calls the dispatch table's end entry (+0x2c, inferred End) and zeroes the pair at +0x80 and +0x84 of the record at +0x6d4. The words at +0x88 and +0x8c of that record take 1.0 and 0 when the byte at +0x19dcd is set, and otherwise a unit step whose sign follows the order of the far and near distances at +0x165d0 and +0x165c8, and that step multiplied by n. When the projection class at +0x19dbc is 3 it also writes four clip planes into the record at +0x6d4, from +0x90 to +0xcc: for the x and y rows of the projection matrix at +0x741c the planes are 0.9 times the row's x (or y) term plus its w term, and w minus 0.9 times the term, so they sit at 1/0.9 of the viewing volume's x and y bounds (the constant is 0.9 in the library's double pool at 0xdaf4fc0). It sets bits 8 to 12 of the clip dirty word at +0x70c and bits 4, 8 and 10 of +0x16964, sets the global at 0x1018 to 2 and the in-replay bytes +0x19d8e and +0x19dc8, and clears the two bytes at the end.
  The replay is a switch on the mode. For each mode it calls the dispatch table's begin entry (+0x1c, inferred Begin) with the mode (a line loop temporarily swaps the entry at +0x19ea0 for the partial soft-clip loop-begin routine around that call), sends each held vertex with `__glOdysseySendShadowAttributes` and `__glOdysseySendShadowVertex`, then the current attributes (index -1) and the argument vertex through the Vertex4f entry at +0x570 (inferred). With fewer than two vertices held every case replays the held ones; with more, the polygon and fan cases replay the oldest held vertex and one chosen by a modulo-3 step of the ring index, the quad cases replay the vertices at ring offsets 1, 2 and 3 from the oldest or the last two, and the strip case with the parity flag set replays two held vertices and the new one as a one-triangle primitive begun in mode 4, ends it, and restarts mode 5 from the last held vertex and the new one.

## Texture

- `__glOdysseyTextureActivate` (`builds/6.5.30-media/usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`, 0xd9d1f90, 1448 bytes): **established.**
  Makes a texture object the one the board samples (arguments: the context and the texture object); returns 1 on success and 0 on failure. It returns 0 at once when the object's board-memory handle at +0x4fc is -1. It takes the last level to bind as the smaller of the object's maximum level (+0x60) and +0x9c (for a 3D object, target 0x806f, the base level at +0x5c), opens a reference to the object in the board-memory manager (`__glOdysseyOpenPMObjReference`, handle, base and last level, and the object's per-level offsets at +0x514) and raises GL error 0x505 (out of memory) when that fails. On success it records the object at +0x16418 and the level range at +0x16420 and +0x16424.
  For each level from the base to the last it writes the three-word level descriptor at +0x7ce0 + 12 x (level - base): word 0 takes the level's board-memory offset from +0x514 + 4 x level into bits 28:0, word 1 takes the format index (+0x554, bits 25:20), the +0x558 field (bits 31:30) and the row length in 16-texel units rounded up to an even number (bits 11:0, with 4096 stored as bit 0), and word 2 takes, for a 3D object, the ceiling of the base-2 logarithm of the row length times the number of rows, both in 16-texel units (bits 11:0), and 0 for other targets. It then derives a 3-bit class from +0x554 and the first level's format at +0x56c with the unnamed routine at 0xd9cfc80 and stores it in bits 25:23 of the object's mode word at +0xb4. For the detail-texture filter modes (the filter field of +0xb4 equal to 2, 3 or 4) it opens a second reference to the detail object at +0x16410 and writes its descriptors from +0x7fe0; a failed open raises error 0x505, deactivates the object and returns 0. It ends by clearing bits 5:0 of the word at +0x7fe0, ORing 0x1c00 into the dirty word at +0x16960 and setting the global at 0x1018 to 2.

- `__glOdysseyValidateTexture` (`builds/6.5.30-media/usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`, 0xd9d26d0, 1236 bytes): **established.**
  Checks one texture object for use by the board and fills the words of its texture-mode block (arguments: the context and the object); its result, stored at +0x10 of the object, is 0 (unusable), 1 (usable) or 2 (needs another validation). After the unnamed routine at 0xd9d2660 it handles the detail-texture magnification filters (the word at +0x18 equal to 0x8097, 0x8098 or 0x8099): for a 2D object whose format at +0x554 matches the detail object's and whose first level is not empty it copies the detail object's two parameters from +0x90 and +0x94 (five bits each) into bits 24:20 and 19:15 of +0xa4, and otherwise it sets the filter field of +0xb4 to 1; for other targets it clears bit 20 of +0xa0 and does the same. It then sets bit 9 of +0xa0.
  It computes the three level counts n = max(0, size level - base level) from +0x90, +0x94 and +0x98 minus the base level at +0x5c, packs them into +0xa4 (bits 14:10, 9:5 and 4:0 for the first, second and third axis), and writes into +0xa8, +0xac and +0xb0 the product of 2^n and the object's clamp floats at +0x48, +0x4c and +0x50, truncated to 17 bits (`docs/buzz.md` has the field reading of these words). A level-0 width, height or depth of zero returns 0. It requires the wrap word at +0x28 to be 0x2901 (GL repeat), calling the unnamed routine at 0xd9d5d20 when it is not (for a 3D object it compares +0x28 with +0x24 instead), and then the result depends on the minification filter at +0x14: for 0x2600, 0x2601 and 0x8146 (nearest, linear and the four-tap filter) a target other than 1D or 2D is valid at once, and a 1D or 2D one asks the unnamed routine at 0xd9d1dc0 and returns 1 on success or, on failure, sets bit 14 of +0xa0, clears +0xcc and returns 2. For the mipmap filters it walks the levels up to the smaller of +0x60 and +0x9c, halving each size (minimum 1, border removed) and comparing every stored level's format, border (+0x5c0 + 0x44 x level) and sizes with what a complete mipmap needs, returning 0 at the first mismatch and 1 when all match.
- `__glOdysseyValidateTextureState` (`builds/6.5.30-media/usr/gfx/arch/IP30ODSY32/libGLcore.so`, 0xd9d2bb0, 1204 bytes): **established.**
  Chooses which of the 1D, 2D and 3D texture objects the board samples and copies that object's board words into the context. The enable word at +0x700 decides: bit 30 selects the 3D object (pointer at +0x1640c), else bit 17 the 2D object (+0x16408), else bit 16 the 1D object (+0x16404). A candidate with the state value 2 at +0x10 is first validated with `__glOdysseyValidateTexture`; one that still has 0 is skipped. The chosen dimension sets bits 5, 4 or 3 (3D, 2D, 1D) and bit 10 in the word at +0x7c00 and bit 2 of the dirty word, and when no object qualifies the routine clears bits 10, 11, 5, 4 and 3 there instead, deactivates any bound object and returns.
  If the chosen object is not the one bound at +0x16418 it ORs 0x11c200 into +0x16960, deactivates the bound object, and activates the new one with `__glOdysseyTextureActivate`; on success it copies the object's words into the context: +0xb4 (masked into +0x7cd4), +0xa4 to +0xb0 into +0x7cc4 to +0x7cd0, +0xb8 and +0xbc into +0x7cd8 and +0x7cdc (the seven words of block 0x16), +0xc0 to +0xcc into +0x9724 to +0x9730 (block 0x13), +0xd0 to +0xd8 into +0x8d6c to +0x8d74 (block 0x3e), the eight post-filter words +0xdc to +0xf8 into +0x8cec to +0x8d08 with the post-filter mode at +0x8ce8 set to 2, and 256 words from +0xfc to +0x87e4 to +0x8be0 (the table sent by dirty bit 20). It ORs the object's own dirty word (+0xa0) into +0x16960, clears it, and sets the global at 0x1018 to 2.
